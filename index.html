Calculation details
Direct shots use ghost-ball geometry. Bank shots use reflected-pocket geometry to calculate the cushion contact point. The route is then checked for ball interference and table boundaries before it is displayed.

This is geometric table guidance. Real results can vary with speed, spin, throw, cloth and cushion conditions.
