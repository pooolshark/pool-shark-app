Shot calculation
The calculator uses a single physical coordinate system for the entire table. Balls are 2.25 inches in diameter and the playing surface is modeled at a 2:1 ratio. Direct shots use ghost-ball geometry. One-rail banks use reflected-target geometry against the actual cushion centerline. The displayed route is generated from the same coordinates used by the solver.

Real-world results can vary with speed, spin, throw, cloth, cushion response, pocket shelf and pocket condition.
