Calculation details
Direct shots use ghost-ball geometry. Bank shots use a reflected-pocket construction to determine the actual cushion contact point. The displayed bank route is checked before it is shown, so the app does not intentionally draw a route through the wrong side of the object ball.

This is ideal table geometry. Actual results can vary with speed, spin, throw, cloth and cushion response.
